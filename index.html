What this is

A structure, not a sentiment. Delta Tau Delta has always been good at making brothers. It has never been consistently good at keeping them connected. This is the connective infrastructure that closes that gap.

Proof of a promise. Every fraternity tells its members that brotherhood lasts a lifetime. Until now, ours had nothing built behind that claim.

A beginning. This is not the unveiling of a finished product. It is supposed to be shaped by the men who join it.

What this isn't

Not a new layer above your work. House corporation members and chapter advisors are already doing this work. The Association is built to give you more support, not more oversight. Central Office enables; alumni own.

Not a fundraising ask. Joining is free, and no decisions on dues have been made. Giving to the Educational Foundation is a separate thing and stays separate.

Not finished. Several things on this page are still being decided, and we would rather say so than pretend otherwise.

Questions

Asked the way you would ask them

My city already has an alumni chapter. Does this compete with it?

No. Alumni chapters are where Delt brotherhood already lives after graduation, and the Association is built to make them stronger, not to stand in for them. The plan is to give chapter events more exposure across the national network, to create a way for chapters to share what is working from one city to the next, and to build a playbook for brothers who want to start a chapter where none exists. The Founding Committee will decide how that support takes shape, and alumni chapter leaders are exactly the brothers it needs to hear from.

Does this replace what my house corporation or chapter advisor already does?

No. You are already doing the work this Association exists to support. Nothing about how your chapter's alumni relationships or house corporation are run changes. What changes is what sits behind you: a national database you can actually use to find your chapter's alumni, communications and event infrastructure you no longer have to build yourself, and a seat at the table where decisions about all of this get made. Central Office enables; alumni own. If it ever starts to feel like oversight, tell us, because that is a sign we have built it wrong.

Is this just fundraising with better branding?

No. Membership is free during the founding period, and nothing on this page asks you for money. No decisions on dues have been made. Whether, when, and how dues are introduced is the Founding Committee's to decide, and members will hear what it decides. Giving to the Educational Foundation is separate, run by a separate organization, and it stays that way.

We have heard promises like this before. Why is this different?

Fair. Several initiatives have launched with fanfare and faded, and we are not going to pretend otherwise. Two things are different this time. First, it is being built in the open: this page tells you what has not been decided yet rather than hiding it. Second, it is not being built by Central Office alone. The Founding Committee is alumni, the decisions are theirs, and their first job is to define what the Association offers alumni, present that to the Arch Chapter, and decide what success looks like and how it gets measured. If that measure is not met, you will know.

I already have my own Delt network. Why do I need this?

You may not, and that is fine. We believe Delta Tau Delta has one of the strongest alumni communities in the fraternity world. It has just never been connected. If your network is already working for you, the Association is not asking you to replace it. It is asking whether the brother who graduated from a chapter you have never visited, in a city you just moved to, has the same thing you have. Most of them do not, and that is the gap this is built to close.

Who runs this Association?

The Founding Committee, seated this fall from more than 100 applications. See who is on it. The Arch Chapter, Central Office, and the Educational Foundation each hold a non-voting liaison seat. The Founding Committee serves a term of about one year. Its reporting cadence is among the first things it will settle, and it will be published here when it is.

What happens to my information?

It stays in myDelt, the Fraternity's existing member system, which is where it already lives. Joining the Association adds a membership record to your existing profile; it does not create a second database or hand your information to anyone outside the Fraternity. If a member directory is built, it will be visible to members only, and you will control what appears in it. Partner benefits, when they exist, will require your opt-in before any partner sees anything about you.
